# The Human OS — Image Requirements

## Visual Theme

All images must follow a consistent visual style:

- **Style:** Clean, minimal flat illustration / line art. No photorealism, no 3D renders.
- **Color palette:** Limited to 4 colors + white background:
  - **Primary (teal/cyan):** `#2B9EB3` — for main structures, flows, positive states
  - **Secondary (warm coral):** `#E8734A` — for highlights, alerts, hormones, active states
  - **Accent (soft gold):** `#F2C14E` — for energy, nutrients, signals
  - **Neutral (dark slate):** `#3A3F47` — for outlines, labels, text
  - **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (light mode safe; keep it transparent or white)
- **Line weight:** Consistent medium-weight strokes, rounded ends
- **Labels:** Short, sans-serif labels directly on the image where needed (use Helvetica/Arial style)
- **Aspect ratio:** Landscape or 3:2 preferred (fits 680px article column well). Suggested size: ~1360×900 px (renders at 680×450 in article).
- **No text-heavy images** — the article text provides context; images should be mostly visual with minimal labeling
- **Format:** PNG with white or transparent background
